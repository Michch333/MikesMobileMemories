Universal Google Form Setup Guide

This storefront uses a Universal 5-Field Mapping Strategy. Add new products in data/catalog.js anytime without ever editing your Google Form or HTML.

  1. Create a Google Form with 5 Short Answer questions at the top: Selected Product, Selected Options / Specs, Quantity, Estimated Price Summary, and Custom Notes.
  2. Add a File Upload question below those so the customer can attach their high-res artwork, plus their Name, Email, and Phone.
  3. In your Google Form editor, click the three dots (top right) → Get pre-filled link, type dummy text into the 5 fields, click Get Link, and copy the entry.XXXXXXX IDs into data/config.js.
